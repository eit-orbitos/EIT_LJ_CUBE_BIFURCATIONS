We submit for your consideration the manuscript **"Symmetry-Breaking Bifurcations from the Cubic Equilibrium in
Two-Term Inverse-Power Clusters"** by Toni Mladenovski and Mihail Mladenski (EITNetworks LLC, Tobyhanna, Pennsylvania,
USA).

The manuscript studies local symmetry-breaking bifurcations from the eight-particle cubic equilibrium in two-term
inverse-power clusters, with pair potential V(r) = A r^(-p) - B r^(-q), a family that contains the Lennard-Jones
potential. It builds on companion work (Paper 2, a Zenodo archive record, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24187155) that classified
the linear (full-vector) spectrum of the cubic equilibrium and identified the transition curves on which its Morse
index changes. The present manuscript derives the local nonlinear branch structure at those curves. The main results
are:

- at the A2u curve, a Z2-symmetric pitchfork of T_d configurations (two concentric regular tetrahedra of unequal radii
  on inversion-related vertex-direction sets), supercritical in the reduced-energy convention;
- at the T2g curve, transcritical branches of D3d symmetry, derived directly from the cubic invariant and the implicit
  function theorem;
- at the same curve, a Z2-symmetric pitchfork of D2h configurations, supercritical in the reduced-energy axial
  convention; the D2h configurations have point group exactly D2h and are not cuboids;
- the inertia of the D2h branch points on the three-dimensional centre space, (1, 0, 2), and in the
  rigid-motion-reduced configuration space, (4, 0, 14): these branch points are saddles.

The scope is local. Global continuation, secondary bifurcations and global energy landscapes are outside the scope of
the manuscript, and no global stability claim and no technological claim is made.

The nonlinear coefficients whose signs decide the branch types are certified on the whole relevant curves with
rigorous computer-assisted ball arithmetic (Arb) and independent checkers; the computer-assisted proofs are
reproducible from the archived code, certificates and tests. Each computational phase also has an independent
computational audit record produced by a separate AI system; these audits are computational verifications, not
journal peer review, and are described as such in the manuscript. An implementation defect found and corrected during
the work (erratum E-B1) is disclosed in full. All code, certificates, checkers, tests and audit records will be
archived at [PAPER 3 ZENODO DOI — TO BE ASSIGNED AT PUBLIC RELEASE].

We are not aware of a prior complete classification of these local branches for this specific eight-particle
two-term inverse-power system; failure to locate prior work is not proof of novelty. Paper 1 (DOI
10.5281/zenodo.24149914) and Paper 2 are Zenodo preprint/archive records; neither is described here as
journal-published.
