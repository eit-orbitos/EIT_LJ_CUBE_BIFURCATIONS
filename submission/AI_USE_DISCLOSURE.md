# AI-use disclosure (for the journal)

The human authors are Toni Mladenovski and Mihail Mladenski; no AI system is an author. AI systems were used as
research tools: ChatGPT (OpenAI; GPT-5.6 Sol), Claude (Anthropic), Kimi (Moonshot AI) and the Tasklet agent execution
environment. They were used for symbolic-derivation assistance, code assistance, test generation, computational
verification, adversarial auditing, manuscript preparation and release preparation. The computations were executed by
AI agents under the direction of Toni Mladenovski, who set every phase gate and takes responsibility for the
scientific claims. No AI workflow is attributed to Mihail Mladenski.

Unverified AI output does not constitute proof, and no mathematical claim rests on it. The claims rest on exact
symbolic calculations, explicit mathematical arguments, standard theorems whose hypotheses are checked, rigorous
interval (ball) arithmetic, independent re-certification, reproducibility records and archived audits. The independent
audits are computational audits performed by an AI system; they are not journal peer review. No figure was produced
with a generative AI tool; all figures are computed by a deterministic script. The full log is `docs/AI_USE_LOG.md` in
the release package.
